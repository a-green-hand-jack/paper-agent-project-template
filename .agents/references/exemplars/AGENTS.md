# Writing and design exemplars

These project-owned records describe materials used to learn presentation, not
works cited as evidence. Use `writing/` for narrative and section examples,
`figures/` for visual examples, and `posters/` for poster examples. Record the
source, identifier, access date, intended use, what is useful and what not to
copy. Register each durable record in the content registry as project-owned.

Keep downloaded third-party PDFs, source bundles and working files under the
worktree's `.project/artifacts/`, or in registered external storage; do not
commit them here. A work used in the manuscript bibliography also needs an
independent citation identity and claim-support review through
`paper-references`; exemplar status alone never supplies scientific evidence.

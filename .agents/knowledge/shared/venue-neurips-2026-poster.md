# NeurIPS 2026: poster facts

## Fact

Checked on 2026-10-20 against the official page under Evidence.

### Poster size

- Main conference: 140 cm wide x 90 cm high, landscape, the same at all three
  locations.
- Workshops: 61 cm x 85 cm, portrait.
- "Do not print your poster larger than what is listed here."

### Print orders

- Sydney: advance order deadline November 4, 11:00 PM AEST; the regular
  deadline is "date coming soon".
- Atlanta: November 25, 11:00 PM PDT.
- Paris: November 30, 11:00 PM CET; pick-up December 9-11 for the conference
  and December 12-13 for workshops.

### Unknowns and conflicts

- The page has no last-updated date, and says "The camera-ready stage is
  currently closed".
- Earlier years used other sizes (main-conference boards of 96 x 48 in, any
  size that fits); a size chosen from an earlier year, such as 48 x 36 in
  (121.9 x 91.4 cm), is now too high for the 90 cm limit.
- NeurIPS 2026 paper page limits and dates are not in this record.

## Evidence

- https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/PosterInstructions, read 2026-10-20.

## Refresh

Recheck the size and the order deadline of the chosen location before
ordering; the page states no update date, so a check older than a week before
an order is stale.

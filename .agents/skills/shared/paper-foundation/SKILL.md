# Paper foundation

Read `PAPER.md` and only the project records needed for the task. Keep Human decisions and the distinction between planned and observed results. A locked claim, experiment, interpretation or interface requires review before changing meaning. Map reported numbers through `paper/macros.tex` and `assets/MANIFEST.yaml`.

For a new paper or session, use the orientation and brief procedures; validate a supplied brief before filling decided fields. For a central decision, prepare alternatives with evidence and record the Human's choice. For recurring names, notation, claims and result macros, update the paper and its interface record together.

# Paper foundation

Read the project records the task needs, starting with `.agents/memory/project/paper-foundation/brief.md`. Keep Human decisions and the distinction between planned and observed results. A locked claim, experiment, interpretation or interface requires review before changing meaning. Map reported numbers through `paper/macros.tex` and `.agents/memory/project/paper-foundation/results-ledger.md`.

For a new paper or session, read the orientation card and fill the brief record; validate a supplied brief before writing decided fields into `.agents/memory/project/paper-foundation/brief.md`. For a central decision, prepare alternatives with evidence and record the Human's choice. For recurring names, notation, claims and result macros, update the paper and its interface record together. Appendix material goes to `paper/appendix/`, one file per appendix ([`appendix-as-its-own-tree`](../../../memory/shared/paper-foundation/appendix-as-its-own-tree.md)).
